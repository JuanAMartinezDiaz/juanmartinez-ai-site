Public comment submitted · 22 September 2026

NIST AI 200-2 — TEVV-Athlon Framework

Submitted comments proposing explicit decision-authority testing for agentic systems, including human-only actions, exact-action binding, revocation, replay resistance, realistic protected-execution testing and evidence sufficient to distinguish proposal, authorization and effect.

Submission — not an endorsement by NIST
Public comment submitted · 22 September 2026

NIST SP 1353 — AI-Assisted CSF Analysis and Reporting

Submitted comments recommending clearer separation between AI-assisted analysis and accountable assurance conclusions, with stronger treatment of evidence sufficiency, provenance, human validation and decision ownership.

Submission — not an endorsement by NIST
