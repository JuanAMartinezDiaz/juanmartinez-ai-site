AFTER · SENTINEL’S PROPOSAL

AI prepares. A person decides.

  1. PERSON · INTAKECapture the dispute once

    Keep the original case details as the shared record.

    BENEFIT · A shared starting point

    Original details stay with the case, reducing the need to reconcile separate copies.

  2. BENEFIT · One duplicate entry task removed

    Reuse the original details; test whether this reduces transcription errors and preparation time.

  3. BENEFIT · Avoid an unnecessary review-and-return cycle

    Catch missing records before manager review instead of discovering the gap later.

  4. BENEFIT · A named person owns the decision

    Keep final authority with the dispute manager; deny unauthorized AI actions.

  5. OPERATIONS · AFTER APPROVALRecord the decision and notify

    Retain the evidence, approver, and reason with the case.

    BENEFIT · A reviewable decision record

    Keep the decision, evidence, and approver together for later review.
