Analytics privacy

Updated October 29, 2026. This notice covers juanmartinez.ai, including its BAI pages. Other domains have their own tracking configuration.

Aggregate traffic

Vercel Web Analytics measures pageviews, daily visitor estimates, referrals, devices, broad geography, and supported campaign dimensions. It uses a daily hash rather than analytics cookies. It cannot recognize the same person across different days or reveal visitors’ names or email addresses.

Optional Google Analytics

If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics property G-BBSDXENED8 receives pageviews and selected link-click events. Browser identifiers support returning-browser, engagement, and campaign reports. These identifiers are pseudonymous, not fully anonymous. They do not tell us your name or email address, and they do not reliably join different devices or browsers. Analytics cookies are configured for 90 days. Your choice is remembered for 180 days.

Google Analytics does not load before you accept. Rejecting optional cookies leaves aggregate Vercel measurement in place. Browser Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track signals disable optional Google Analytics. Use “Analytics settings” to change your choice; rejecting after acceptance clears this site's Google Analytics cookies and reloads the page. Data previously sent is not erased by withdrawing consent.

Campaigns and inquiries

Campaign links may carry UTM source, medium, campaign, content, and term labels. Do not include personal or confidential information in URLs or campaign labels. Selected click events record a page path and a destination category or hostname, not email-message contents. An email click measures intent; it does not prove that an inquiry was sent or a sale occurred.

No advertising pixels, LinkedIn Insight Tag, remarketing, Google signals, or advertising personalization are enabled by this implementation. Advertising spend, impressions, and sales must be reconciled separately to measure return on advertising spend. Google Analytics is an optional third-party analytics service.
