Before we begin

A few practical questions.

What can I explore today?

You can explore the synthetic before-and-after workflow demo and the separate Decision X-Ray reference demonstration, then discuss an assessment with Juan. The demonstrations illustrate the approach; they are not a production deployment of Sentinel.

Will the assessment prove savings or compliance?

It identifies opportunities and defines how a pilot should measure outcomes. Demo figures are illustrative estimates. Savings, compliance conclusions, and operating effectiveness need appropriate evidence from the actual workflow; they are not promised.

What happens after I get in touch?

Juan reviews your general workflow summary, arranges a discovery conversation, and confirms fit. A written scope sets the deliverables, fee, schedule, payment terms, data handling, and acceptance before paid work begins.

What information should I send?

Start with your organization, role, and a general description of the workflow. Do not send account credentials, customer records, or confidential documents. Sensitive materials should be shared only after scope and a suitable handling method are agreed.
