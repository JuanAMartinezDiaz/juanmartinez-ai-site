Questions before getting started

Is Sentinel a production platform I can deploy today?

No. The public reference demo illustrates a bounded governance pattern. Production use requires separate runtime validation, security and operating controls, and engagement-specific acceptance.

Will the assessment prove savings or compliance?

The assessment identifies opportunities and defines how a pilot should measure outcomes. Savings, compliance conclusions, and operating effectiveness must be established with appropriate evidence; they are not promised.

What happens after I inquire?

Juan reviews your non-sensitive process summary, arranges a discovery conversation, and confirms fit. A written scope identifies deliverables, schedule, payment terms, data handling, and acceptance before paid work begins.
