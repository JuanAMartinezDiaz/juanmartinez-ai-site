Operating rules

The work remains yours.

The AI Build Lab is designed around a simple boundary: AI may help frame, compare, draft, test, and organize. It does not inherit accountability because it produced fluent output.

  • Use real evidence or clearly labeled synthetic information.
  • Do not paste secrets, confidential work material, health information, or data you lack permission to share.
  • Verify consequential claims at their original source.
  • Keep approval, disclosure, and final decisions with a responsible person.
